The Indian Premier League (IPL) currently features 10 franchises. These teams represent major cities and are highly professionalized cricket units that participate in the seasonal tournament.

Here is the list of all IPL teams:
1. Mumbai Indians (MI)
2. Chennai Super Kings (CSK)
3. Royal Challengers Bangalore (RCB)
4. Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR)
5. Rajasthan Royals (RR)
6. Sunrisers Hyderabad (SRH)
7. Gujarat Titans (GT)
8. Punjab Kings (PBKS) - *Note: Franchise status may change.*
9. Delhi Capitals/Delhi Daredevils (DC)
10. Lucknow Super Giants (LSG)

**Disclaimer:** This list is based on the recognized franchises participating in recent and projected IPL seasons. The teams can occasionally undergo name or ownership changes, so always verify with official cricket sources for the most current information.
