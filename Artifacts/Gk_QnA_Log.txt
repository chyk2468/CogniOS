General Knowledge Question Log
=============================

1. What is the capital of India?
   Answer: New Delhi. 

2. How many colours are there in a rainbow?
   Answer: Seven colors. 

3. Who was the first Prime Minister of India?
   Answer: Jawaharlal Nehru. 

4. Which is the largest mammal on Earth?
   Answer: Blue whale. 

5. How many days are there in a week?
   Answer: Seven days. 

6. What is the boiling point of water?
   Answer: 100 degrees Celsius. 

7. Which bird is known as a symbol of peace?
   Answer: Dove. 

8. Who built the Sun Temple at Konark?
   Answer: Narasimhadeva I. 

9. Who authored Indica, describing Mauryan India?
   Answer: Megasthenes. 

10. The ancient text “Silappadikaram” is part of the literature of which region?
    Answer: Tamil literature. 

11. Which biomolecule acts as the primary “genetic material” in most viruses?
    Answer: RNA or DNA. 

12. Which sea is the only one on Earth that has no land coasts?
    Answer: Sargasso Sea. 

13. What is the only national flag in the world that does not have four sides?
    Answer: Nepal. 

14. Which newly realized exotic state of matter confirms a nearly 50-year-old prediction by directly exhibiting measurable topological order without conventional symmetry breaking?
    Answer: Fractional quantum Hall effect.
